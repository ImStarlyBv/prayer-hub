Lord Jesus, Divine Physician, I come before You in my weakness and ask for Your healing touch.

Touch what is broken in my body, ease what troubles my mind, and calm what is heavy in my spirit.

Where there is pain, grant relief. Where there is fear, grant peace. Where there is despair, grant hope.

Not my will, but Yours be done. I trust that by Your wounds I am healed.

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.
