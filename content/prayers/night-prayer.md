O Lord, as the day now ends and darkness falls upon the earth, I come before You to give thanks for the hours that have passed and to place myself under Your watchful care through the night.

Forgive me the faults of this day, both known and unknown. Grant rest to my body and peace to my soul, that I may rise tomorrow renewed in Your grace.

Watch over those I love, guard this household from harm, and let Your holy angels stand as sentinels at my door until the light of morning comes.

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.
