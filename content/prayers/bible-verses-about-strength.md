There are days when our own strength runs out long before the day does. Scripture speaks honestly about weariness, and then points us to a strength that does not come from ourselves.

"The LORD is my strength and my shield; my heart trusted in him, and I am helped" (Psalm 28:7). Strength here is not something we work up — it is something we receive from leaning on Him.

Isaiah promised, "They that wait upon the LORD shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary" (Isaiah 40:31). Waiting on Him is where renewal happens.

Lord, my strength is small today. Renew me as only You can, and remind me that I do not have to carry it alone. Amen.
