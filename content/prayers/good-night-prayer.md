Lord, the day is done. I lay down its burdens at Your feet and give You thanks for every mercy I received, seen and unseen.

Bless my sleep, quiet my mind, and keep my heart at peace. Watch over my family and all who are dear to me this night.

Good night, Lord. Into Your hands I commend my spirit. Amen.
