Almighty God, my refuge and my fortress, I come to You asking for Your protection over my life.

Shield me from harm, from accident, and from the schemes of the enemy. Surround me with Your holy angels wherever I go today.

Protect my family, my home, and all that You have entrusted to me. Let no evil take hold where Your grace has already made a dwelling.

I trust in You, my God, my strength, in whom I take refuge. Amen.
