Saint Michael the Archangel, defender in the day of battle, I come to you asking for your protection.

Be my shield against the snares and wickedness of the enemy, who prowls about seeking to bring harm where he can. Stand watch over my home, my family, and my heart.

Give me courage where I am afraid, and clarity where I am confused about what is right. Help me to stand firm in what is good, even when it is hard.

By the power of God, cast out every shadow of evil that seeks to unsettle my peace. Let no fear take root that does not come from Him.

Saint Michael, prince of the heavenly host, watch over me and all who call on your protection.

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.
