Heavenly Father, I thank You for the gift of this new day. Before I take a single step, I offer You my thoughts, my words, and my work.

Guide my steps, guard my tongue, and keep my heart set on what is good. Let everything I do this day be done for Your glory and in service of those around me.

Where I am weak, be my strength. Where I am afraid, be my courage. Go before me and prepare the way.

I ask this through Christ our Lord. Amen.
