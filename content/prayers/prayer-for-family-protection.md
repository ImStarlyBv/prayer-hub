Lord God, Father of every family, I lift up my household to You this day.

Guard my spouse, my children, and every member of my family, wherever they may be. Protect them from sickness, from accident, and from all danger to body and soul.

Bind us together in Your love, and let no discord or harm come between us. Send Your angels to walk beside each one of us today.

Through the intercession of the Holy Family, hear our prayer. Amen.
